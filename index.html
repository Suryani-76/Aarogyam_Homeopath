Conditions & Areas of Care

We provide supportive clinical assessment and personalized homeopathic care for chronic and recurrent concerns without unverified claims or guaranteed outcomes.

Skin & Hair Concerns

Dermatological Assessment

Careful evaluation for psoriasis, eczema, allergic dermatitis, and chronic scalp or skin complaints. Prescriptions are tailored to constitutional triggers and flare patterns.

Focuses on gentle constitutional balance and barrier support.

Digestive & Gut Concerns

Gastrointestinal Support

Assessment of recurrent digestive symptoms, acidity, bloating, irritable bowel tendencies, and sluggish metabolism with careful attention to diet and personal medical history.

Integrates dietary modifications with individualized remedies.

Kidney & Urinary Concerns

Renal Supportive Care

Supportive homeopathic consultation for recurrent urinary symptoms and chronic complaints. Patients with suspected kidney stones require formal diagnostic evaluation.

Severe colic pain, fever, or urinary obstruction requires immediate hospital evaluation.

Metabolic & Lifestyle Concerns

Complementary Support

Supportive guidance for patients living with diabetes, thyroid irregularities, or metabolic fatigue. Designed to work alongside your regular medical routine.

Prescribed diabetes treatment and blood glucose monitoring must never be stopped without your treating physician's consent.

Stress, Sleep & Wellbeing

Counselling & Homeopathy

Dr. Harini's background in Psychotherapy provides supportive counselling-oriented sessions for chronic emotional stress, anxiety coping, and non-restorative sleep patterns.

Conducted within qualified scope of clinical psychotherapy and homeopathy.

Acute & Chronic Complaints

Long-term Health Management

Personalized evaluation for recurrent respiratory sensitivities, joint stiffness, headaches, and prolonged health concerns where long-term management is sought.

Each plan is reviewed on a scheduled basis to assess ongoing response.
